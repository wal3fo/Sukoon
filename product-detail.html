The Essence of Clarity

In the medina of Meknes, where the air carries the scent of spice and earth, mint grows wild and proud. Generations of farmers have tended these fields, knowing exactly when to harvest — just before the sun reaches its peak, when the essential oils are at their most potent.

Menthe Marocaine captures that moment. Each pyramid sachet contains whole, unbroken leaves that unfurl slowly in hot water, releasing a vivid green liquor and an aroma that instantly transports you to a Moroccan courtyard at noon.

This isn't just tea. It's a ritual of pause. The menthol opens your airways. The brightness clears your mind. In a world of noise, this is your signal to focus.

Origin

Sourced from family farms in the Meknes-Tafilalet region of Morocco, where the unique microclimate — cool Atlas Mountain nights and intense Mediterranean days — produces mint with exceptionally high menthol content and a naturally sweet finish.

Our farming partners practice regenerative agriculture, rotating mint with nitrogen-fixing legumes to maintain soil health without synthetic inputs.
